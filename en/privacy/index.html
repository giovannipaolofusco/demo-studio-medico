1. Controller

Giovanni Paolo Fusco, who can be contacted at giovanni@giovannipaolofusco.it. No data protection officer (DPO) has been appointed.

2. What data is processed

The website has no forms, does not ask you to register and does not use cookies. The only personal data processed is browsing data: IP address, date and time of the request, the page requested and the browser type. It is recorded automatically by the hosting provider, as is the case for any website.

3. Purposes and legal basis

Browsing data is used to serve the pages you request and to protect the website from abuse and cyberattacks. The legal basis is the controller's legitimate interest in the operation and security of the website (Article 6(1)(f) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the “Regulation”).

4. Recipients

The data is processed by Cloudflare, Inc., which hosts the website, as a data processor under its data processing agreement. Cloudflare may entrust parts of the service to other processors (sub-processors), listed at cloudflare.com/gdpr/subprocessors. The data is not disclosed to anyone else or made public.

5. Transfers outside the European Union

Cloudflare is based in the United States. The transfer relies on Cloudflare's participation in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795, an adequacy decision under Article 45 of the Regulation) and on the standard contractual clauses adopted by the European Commission (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914), which are part of its data processing agreement. Some of Cloudflare's sub-processors are also located outside the European Union. A copy of the standard contractual clauses is available in Cloudflare's data processing agreement, cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa, or on request from the controller.

6. How long data is kept

Browsing data stays in the hosting provider's technical logs for as long as needed to keep the service secure, under the provider's own policies, described at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy. The controller does not keep copies.

7. Cookies and browser storage

The website does not use cookies. It uses the browser's session storage (sessionStorage, key apertura-vista) only to remember that the opening animation has already been shown: this is technical storage needed for the website to work as designed, it contains no personal data and it is deleted when you close the tab. For this reason it does not require consent (Article 122(1) of Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003) and no banner is needed.

8. Do you have to provide your data?

Browsing data is needed to visit the website. No other data is requested.

9. Your rights

You can ask the controller for access to your data, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing (Articles 15–18 of the Regulation) by writing to giovanni@giovannipaolofusco.it. As the controller does not keep browsing data, requests concerning the provider's logs will be handled through Cloudflare.

Right to object. You may object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on legitimate interests (Article 21 of the Regulation).

None of the processing on this website is based on consent, so there is no consent to withdraw.

10. Automated decision-making

There is no automated decision-making, including profiling, referred to in Article 22(1) and (4) of the Regulation.

11. Complaints

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Article 77 of the Regulation), in particular in the Member State of your habitual residence, place of work or place of the alleged infringement. In Italy, the supervisory authority is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali; see garanteprivacy.it for how to lodge a complaint.

12. Children

None of the processing on this website is based on consent, so the rules on children's consent (Article 8 of the Regulation and Article 2-quinquies of Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003) do not apply. The only data processed is browsing data, which is the same for every visitor. A parent or holder of parental responsibility can exercise the rights in section 9 on the child's behalf by writing to giovanni@giovannipaolofusco.it.

13. Changes to this notice and new purposes

If this notice changes, the updated version is published on this page with a new date. Before using browsing data for a purpose other than those described here, the controller will state the new purpose, together with any other relevant information, on this page and through a notice on the website.

14. Editing area

The /admin/ area is used only by the controller to update opening hours and notices, with access through a GitHub account. It is not intended for visitors and collects no visitor data. The panel stores some display preferences and the login session in the browser of the person using it (localStorage): they stay on that device and are used only for the panel to work.

15. Contact

For any question about this notice or your data: Giovanni Paolo Fusco, giovanni@giovannipaolofusco.it.
