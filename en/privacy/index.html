Who actually processes the data on this website

Studio Medico Esempio does not exist: this is a demo website. This notice describes the real processing of the website's browsing data, whose controller is the person who built and publishes it: Giovanni Paolo Fusco, giovanni@giovannipaolofusco.it.

1. Data controller

Giovanni Paolo Fusco, who can be contacted at giovanni@giovannipaolofusco.it. No data protection officer (DPO) has been appointed.

2. What data is processed

The website has no forms, does not ask you to register and does not use cookies. The only personal data processed is browsing data: IP address, date and time of the request, the page requested and the browser type. It is recorded automatically by the hosting provider, as happens on any website.

3. Purposes and legal basis

Browsing data is used to serve the pages you request and to protect the website from abuse and cyber attacks. The legal basis is the controller's legitimate interest in the operation and security of the website (Article 6(1)(f) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679).

4. Recipients

The data is processed by Cloudflare, Inc., which hosts the website, as a data processor under its data processing agreement. It is not disclosed to anyone else or made public.

5. Transfers outside the European Union

Cloudflare is based in the United States. The transfer relies on Cloudflare's participation in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission (Decision 2021/914), which are part of its data processing agreement.

6. How long data is kept

Browsing data stays in the hosting provider's technical logs for as long as needed to keep the service secure, under the provider's own policies. The controller does not keep copies.

7. Cookies and browser storage

The website does not use cookies. It uses the browser's session storage (sessionStorage) only to remember that the opening animation has already been shown: this is technical information, it does not identify anyone and it is deleted when you close the tab. For this reason no consent banner is needed.

8. Do you have to provide your data?

Browsing data is needed to visit the website. No other data is requested.

9. Your rights

You can ask the controller for access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, and you can object to the processing (Articles 15-22 of the Regulation), by writing to giovanni@giovannipaolofusco.it. None of the processing on this website is based on consent, so there is no consent to withdraw. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (Article 77), via garanteprivacy.it.

10. Editing area

The /admin/ area is used only by the controller to update opening hours and notices, with access through a GitHub account. It is not intended for visitors and collects no visitor data.
